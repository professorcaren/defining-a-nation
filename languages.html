Languages of India

Estimated language distribution in India, 1946

Language Percent Region Notes
Hindi 31% North/Northwest Linguistically similar to Urdu, but historically associated with Hindus.
Bengali 17% Bengal Spoken by Hindus and Muslims in the Bengal region, although with some differences.
Punjabi 7% Punjab Predominant language spoken by Sikhs and Muslims in and around the Punjab.
Telugu 5% Hyderabad, Andhra Primarily Hindu speakers.
Marathi 5% Western India Primarily Hindu speakers.
Tamil 4% Southern India Primarily Hindu speakers.
Urdu 4% North/Northwest Linguistically similar to Hindi, but historically associated with Muslims. Prominent in Kashmir.
Gujarati 4% Western India Primarily Hindu speakers.
Malayalam 3% Southern India Hindu, Christian and Muslim speakers.
Kannada 3% Southern India Primarily Hindu speakers.
Oriya 2% Eastern India Primarily Hindu speakers.
Pashto 1% North-West Frontier Province Muslim region bordering Afghanistan.
Other 14%

Censuses of the period often counted Hindi and Urdu together as Hindustani, so the split between them here is an estimate.
