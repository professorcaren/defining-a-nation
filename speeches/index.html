The Archive

Speeches, writings and documents from a century of argument over India’s future, all checked against the printed sources.

  • Speeches and writings (essays, articles, statements, letters, resolutions) each come with a short introduction.
  • Sourcebook readings are the excerpts assigned to each faction, most of them from Sources of Indian Traditions, with that volume’s own headnotes.

Use them as models for your own speeches and as evidence of what each side argued. Page numbers in brackets are those of the printed source, for citation. Your role page lists the texts chosen for your faction; the Primary Sources page lists every faction’s.
